## Alternative / Supplemental Monetization

### Option 1: Media.net (Yahoo/Bing contextual ads)
- Can run parallel to AdSense or as fallback
- RPMs in utility niche: $1–$5
- Approval faster than AdSense in many cases

### Option 2: Carbon Ads / EthicalAds
- Developer-focused ad network
- Flat CPM ~$20 but requires developer/tech audience
- Low fit for general unit converter traffic

### Option 3: Affiliate Links (contextual)
- Example: length/weight converters → link to Amazon scales, rulers, measuring tape
- Low conversion probability for utility queries
- Not worth engineering time at this stage

### Option 4: Ezoic
- AI-optimized ad placement
- Requires 10k+ monthly sessions (or LEAP program for smaller sites)
- RPM uplift vs raw AdSense: 20–50% reported
- Worth evaluating at 20k+ sessions/month

---

## Recommendation

**Primary:** Proceed with AdSense. At 20k sessions, realistic revenue is $80/month with near-zero opex — the unit economics work.

**Faster path while AdSense is pending:** Apply to Media.net immediately. Approval is typically faster and it can serve ads on the same inventory within days.

**At scale (50k+ sessions):** Migrate to Ezoic or test Mediavine RPM optimization to push toward the $8 RPM ceiling.
